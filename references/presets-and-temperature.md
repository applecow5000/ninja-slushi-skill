# Presets & Temperature Settings

The Ninja Slushi has **5 presets** that use RapidChill Technology to hit the
ideal temperature for each drink type. Each preset starts at an optimal default
temperature; the user then fine-tunes with the **Temperature Control (1–10
bars)**.

- **Bottom arrow → lower level → warmer, thinner, sippable, dispenses smoother.**
- **Top arrow → higher level → colder, thicker.**

The **Temperature Progress LEDs pulse** while chilling and go **solid + beep**
when ready. The unit keeps running afterward to hold the drink at temperature.

## Ideal texture per drink (quick reference)

"Ideal texture" is really one target — a sippable-yet-scoopable slush. The **bar
level differs by drink so each one reaches that same ideal texture**: more
sugar/alcohol lowers the freezing point, so it needs a colder (higher) bar. Use
these as the ideal-texture starting point, then nudge to taste.

**Scale:** 1 = warmest/thinnest (most sippable) · 10 = coldest/thickest.

| Preset | Drink | Ideal bars |
|---|---|---|
| **SLUSH** | Sports/energy drinks, sweetened iced tea, kombucha, lemonade, limeade | 1 |
| **SLUSH** | Prepared-drink combos (peach iced tea, cherry limeade, etc.) | 2 |
| **SLUSH** | Soda, fruit punch | 3 |
| **SLUSH** | Sparkling grape/cider (mimosa mocktail) | 8 |
| **FROZEN JUICE** | Cranberry, orange, mango, watermelon, tropical juice | 1 |
| **FROZEN JUICE** | Apple juice / apple cider | 2 |
| **FROZEN JUICE** | Bottled smoothies, pineapple juice | 3 |
| **FROZEN JUICE** | Grape juice, cherry juice | 5 |
| **SPIKED SLUSH** | Hard seltzers | 3 |
| **SPIKED SLUSH** | Sour beers, hard tea, hard cider | 5 |
| **SPIKED SLUSH** | Hard kombucha, hard lemonade, Paloma, Moscow Mule | 6 |
| **SPIKED SLUSH** | Frosé | 8 |
| **SPIKED SLUSH** | Rum & Cola, wine (white or rosé) | 9 |
| **SPIKED SLUSH** | Premixed margaritas | 10 |
| **MILKSHAKE / FRAPPÉ** | Dairy shakes / coffee frappés | preset default (adjust ±1) |

**Texture bands:**
- **Bars 1–3** → light, sippable, pourable slush (thin juices, low-sugar drinks,
  most sodas).
- **Bars 4–6** → classic scoopable slush (juice concentrates, mid-strength spiked
  drinks).
- **Bars 7–10** → dense, thick frozen (wine, spirits, sugar-heavy premixed
  cocktails — they need the extra cold just to hold shape).

> **Community caveat:** the most common real-world complaint is drinks coming out
> **too icy** — the fix is usually **one bar lower**, not higher. The manual's
> "not slushing? go colder" applies to *under*-freezing; an icy/snow-cone result
> means over-concentrated or too cold. Treat the numbers above as starting points
> and adjust warmer if you get ice crystals. See
> [community-troubleshooting.md](community-troubleshooting.md).

## Recommended starting bar level by drink

These are the manufacturer's suggested Temperature Control levels. Start here,
then adjust to taste.

### SLUSH preset — non-alcoholic, non-dairy, sugary drinks
Total time: 15 min (2–3 servings) / 30 min (4–6) / 45 min (6–8).

| Drink | Set bars to |
|---|---|
| Sports drinks, energy drinks, sweetened iced tea, kombucha, lemonade, limeade | **1 bar** |
| Soda, fruit punch | **3 bars** |

### SPIKED SLUSH preset — alcoholic drinks
Total time: 30 min (2–3) / 40 min (4–6) / 50 min (6–8).

| Drink | Set bars to |
|---|---|
| Hard seltzers | **3 bars** |
| Sour beers, hard tea, hard cider | **5 bars** |
| Hard kombucha, hard lemonade, Paloma, Moscow Mule | **6 bars** |
| Rum & Cola, wine (white or rosé) | **9 bars** |
| Premixed margaritas | **10 bars** |

*Higher alcohol content generally needs a higher (colder) level to freeze —
that's why premixed margaritas sit at the top of the range.*

### FROZEN JUICE preset — 100% juices & premade smoothies
Total time: 15 min (2–3) / 25 min (4–6) / 35 min (6–8).

| Drink | Set bars to |
|---|---|
| Cranberry, orange, mango, watermelon, tropical juice | **1 bar** |
| Apple juice / apple cider | **2 bars** |
| Bottled premade smoothies, pineapple juice | **3 bars** |
| Grape juice, cherry juice | **5 bars** |

### MILKSHAKE & FRAPPÉ presets
For dairy/creamy bases and coffee drinks respectively. Full base ratios are in
[official-recipes.md](official-recipes.md) (the "Create Your Own Milkshake &
Frappé" chart). Quick reference:
- **MILKSHAKE base (2–3 servings):** 2 cups whole milk + 1 cup heavy cream +
  1 tsp vanilla + 2 tbsp sugar + ¼ cup flavored syrup/sauce. Or use flavored milk
  powders / flavored milk (+ heavy cream + sugar).
- **FRAPPÉ base (2–3 servings):** a store-bought coffee drink (2¾ cups) + 2 tbsp
  heavy cream; or half & half + black coffee + sugar (+ vanilla/caramel).
- **Both require ≥24 oz and dispensing within 30 min** of the preset ending to
  avoid a foamy output. Sugar/fat matter: too little fat is the #1 cause of
  milkshake/frappé texture problems (community confirms heavy cream over
  half-and-half).

## General principles

- **Default first:** every preset auto-starts at its optimal temperature. Only
  adjust if you want it thinner or thicker.
- **Thinner = smoother dispense.** If a drink is hard to dispense, lower the
  level a notch.
- **Thicker = colder, holds shape.** Raise the level for a firmer slush.
- **Match the preset to the base**, not just the flavor: a *hard* lemonade goes
  on SPIKED SLUSH, a regular lemonade goes on SLUSH.

*Colors, components, and functions may vary by model.*
