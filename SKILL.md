# Ninja Slushi — Countertop Frozen Drink Maker

A home countertop appliance (Ninja markets it as the "Slushi Professional Frozen
Drink Maker," but it's a consumer device, not commercial equipment).

Helps a user operate the Ninja Slushi (FS300/FS301 Series) and get great frozen
drinks every time. Covers the 5 presets, the 1–10 temperature/texture control,
recipes, the sugar & alcohol rules the machine enforces, cleaning, and
troubleshooting.

## The machine in one paragraph

Pour 16–64 oz (2–8 cups) of liquid into the vessel through the easy-fill port,
pick a preset, and RapidChill Technology freezes and continuously churns it with
an auger into slush. Each preset starts at an optimal default temperature; the
user fine-tunes texture with a 1–10 bar Temperature Control. The unit beeps and
holds LEDs solid when the drink is ready, then keeps running to hold it at
temperature until dispensed. **Capacity: 16 oz minimum, 64 oz maximum.**

## Core operating steps

1. Add liquid(s) through the easy-fill port (16 oz min / 64 oz max).
2. Press the power button to turn on.
3. Select a preset to start.
4. If desired, adjust the temperature (see below).
5. Unit beeps and LEDs stay solid once the drink reaches temperature.
6. Dispense and enjoy.

> **Tip:** Chill the liquid before adding it — it reaches slush faster.
> **Note:** For easier cleaning, do NOT turn off the preset until all frozen
> drink has been dispensed.

## The 5 presets

RapidChill picks the ideal temperature per preset. Match the preset to the drink:

| Preset | For | Typical time (2–3 / 4–6 / 6–8 servings) |
|---|---|---|
| **SLUSH** | Non-dairy, non-alcoholic sugary drinks: soda, sports/energy drinks, iced tea, kombucha, lemonade, limeade, fruit punch | 15 / 30 / 45 min |
| **SPIKED SLUSH** | Alcoholic drinks: hard seltzer, wine, premixed cocktails, hard tea/cider/kombucha | 30 / 40 / 50 min |
| **FROZEN JUICE** | 100% juices and premade smoothies | 15 / 25 / 35 min |
| **MILKSHAKE** | Dairy-based milkshakes | see Inspiration Guide chart |
| **FRAPPÉ** | Coffee/blended frappé drinks | see Inspiration Guide chart |

## Temperature / texture control (1–10 bars)

Every preset starts at its optimal default. Then adjust:

- **Lower the level** (bottom arrow) → warmer, thinner, **sippable** slush that
  **dispenses more smoothly**.
- **Raise the level** (top arrow) → colder, **thicker** slush.

Recommended starting bar level for common drinks is in
[references/presets-and-temperature.md](references/presets-and-temperature.md) —
consult it whenever the user names a specific drink.

## Sugar & alcohol rules (the machine enforces these)

The Slushi needs sugar to freeze into slush (and caps alcohol so it can freeze at
all). It actively alerts on out-of-range inputs. **This trips people up
constantly**, so read [references/sugar-alcohol-and-alerts.md](references/sugar-alcohol-and-alerts.md)
whenever a drink involves diet/sugar-free bases, spirits, or a high/low-sugar or
high-alcohol alert (flashing LEDs + beeping every minute for 15 minutes).

Two rules to know up front:
- **Diet soda / sugar-substitute drinks will NOT work** — they lack the sugar
  needed to form slush.
- High-alcohol or high-sugar inputs must be **diluted** to freeze.

## Troubleshooting

"Not slushing" and "not dispensing" fixes are in
[references/cleaning-and-troubleshooting.md](references/cleaning-and-troubleshooting.md).
Quick hits from the manual:
- **Not slushing after 60 min?** Raise temperature one level, wait 10–15 min,
  repeat until desired texture.
- **Not dispensing well?** Fill to the max line, keep the unit running, and lower
  the temperature (more sippable = smoother dispense).

> **Important nuance from the community (see below):** diagnose bad texture by
> *cause*, not by reflexively going colder. A mix that spins as cold **liquid**
> is usually **over-concentrated** (too much sugar/alcohol) → add water. A mix
> that freezes into hard **snow/ice** and knocks is usually **under-sugared** →
> add sugar. Going colder on an over-sugared/over-boozed batch never fixes it.
