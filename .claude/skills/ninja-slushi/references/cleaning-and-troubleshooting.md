# Cleaning, Rinse Cycle & Troubleshooting

## What's in the box / parts

- **Motor base** with attached **evaporator** (the cold surface the drink freezes
  against)
- **Vessel** (475 ml min / 1.9 L max liquid capacity)
- **Auger** (churns the slush)
- **Bail handle**
- **Easy-fill port & cover** (top) and **comfort handle**
- **Pour spout** with removable shroud
- **2-part drip tray** — stores on the side of the unit by sliding onto the knob
- **Condensation catch**
- **Control panel**

## First-time setup & assembly

- **Keep the unit upright for at least 2 hours before first use** (lets the
  refrigerant settle) — skipping this is a common cause of a new unit not cooling.
- Assembly order: level the motor base → install condensation catch (slide into
  rail grooves under the evaporator) → install auger (slide over evaporator onto
  the pin) → slide vessel over auger/evaporator with bail handle up → push bail
  handle back to seal → insert drip tray until it clicks.
- **Stopping the unit stops the auger and cooling** — keep it running to dispense.

## Rinse cycle (run after every use)

The **Rinse cycle agitates without cooling** to rinse the unit. Steps:

1. Dispense any remaining frozen drink.
2. Stop the current preset. Press the **RINSE** button.
3. Add hot water up to the vessel's max fill line (1.9 L).
4. Slowly dispense the water from the unit **immediately after filling**.
5. Stop the Rinse cycle by pressing the **RINSE** button again.
6. Press the power button to turn off the unit.

- **Note:** the water dispenses quickly — use a large cup or bowl to catch it.
- **Note:** repeat if needed until frozen drink is rinsed off the evaporator.
- **After rinsing, wash all parts by hand or in the dishwasher** to fully clean
  the unit after each use.

### Disassembly & washing (official)
1. Unlock the bail handle (pull forward), remove the vessel — **tip it down** so
   residual liquid doesn't spill out the back. Set aside.
2. Slide the auger off the evaporator.
3. Wipe the evaporator with a sanitized or warm, damp cloth.
4. Slide out the condensation catch (may hold liquid). Set aside.
5. Wipe the unit underneath the evaporator.
6. Remove drip tray and/or spout shroud if needed.
7. Hand-wash all parts in hot soapy water, or dishwasher.
8. Wipe the motor base and evaporator with a damp cloth.
9. Let all parts dry fully before reassembling/storing.

- **All parts except the motor base and evaporator are bottom-rack dishwasher
  safe.** Do **NOT** use a heated dry cycle.
- The motor base and evaporator are **wipe-clean only** — never submerge them.
- **Milkshake/Frappé:** dispense within 30 minutes of the preset ending to avoid
  foamy output (and easier cleanup).

> For easier cleaning, **do NOT turn off the preset until all frozen drink has
> been dispensed.**

## Troubleshooting

### Recipe not slushing?
If the recipe hasn't reached the texture you want after **60 minutes**:
1. Use the Temperature Control to **increase the temperature one level**.
2. Wait **10–15 minutes** and test.
3. If still not right, increase by one more level.
4. Repeat until the desired texture is achieved.

Also check the fundamentals:
- Is there enough **sugar**? (Diet/sugar-free won't slush — see
  `sugar-alcohol-and-alerts.md`.)
- Is the **alcohol** too high? Dilute it.
- Did you get a **Low-Sugar** or **High-Alcohol/High-Sugar alert**? Follow its
  fix.
- Chilling the liquid before adding speeds things up.

### Slush not dispensing?
- **Fill to the max fill line (1.9 L)** and make sure the **unit is running**.
- For a smoother dispense, **decrease the temperature level** (bottom arrow) —
  more sippable slush flows out more smoothly.

### Common causes at a glance

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Won't thicken / stays liquid | not enough sugar, or too much alcohol | add sugar (mixed in before pouring) or dilute; raise temp level |
| Beeping + LEDs flashing ascending | High-Alcohol/High-Sugar alert | add 60 ml diluent per serving |
| Beeping + LEDs flashing descending | Low-Sugar alert | add 15–30 ml (or ~12–25 g) sugar per serving |
| Hard/slow to dispense | too thick, or under-filled | lower temp level; fill to max; keep running |
| Never slushes at all | diet/sugar-free base | switch to a full-sugar base |
